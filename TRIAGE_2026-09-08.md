## The ones that need more than a line

### 23 / 12 — the herald-home is not PRR's station

Two reports, one fault. On LPF (and 18XX+) PRR's home is a herald printed on H12 rather than a token, and
the client's state carries it as `home_hex_label: "H12"` with `station_tokens: [[4,7,0]]` — the token PRR placed
elsewhere at index 144. Nothing in `station_tokens` says H12 is PRR's, so:

- `trackReach` / `stationPlacement` find no PRR presence at H12 → every open city lights up (**12**), because
  connectivity is computed from an empty network and the fallback is "anywhere".
- the route planner sees Altoona as a one-slot city PRR is *not* in → #808's bypass rule fires and the run
  goes round it (`bypass: true` at 145 and 147), losing the $10 (**23**).

**The fix is one fact in one place:** the herald-home must be a station for reach and routing purposes (and
never for the token limit or the slot). Whether that is a synthetic entry in `station_tokens` with a flag, or
a `herald_home` field the three readers consult, is the design call — the second is #232-safe for old logs.
Reducer change → port to the server; wants a `JUNO-CV4` replay case that asserts the 145 route is priced at
$120 with H12 counted.

### 18 — derived actions stopped short, twice, on C&O's turn

- **130–132:** player `AdvanceOperatingSubPhase`, `LayTile`, then **one** derived advance and silence. C&O now
  owned a 3-train (bought at 76), so Routes is a real step and stopping *there* is right — but the report
  says the bar stayed on **Lay Track**. So the client drew the pre-burst board: the same live-vs-history read
  #1238 fixed for the payout modal, or the settle-point frame arriving without the derived entry. Reproduce
  with the console on `turn-gate` and the frame kind logged.
- **108–109:** C&O skipped Routes without running, and Dividends was **not** auto-withheld — index 109 is a
  hand-sent `DeclareDividends $0`. `earnableRevenueVerdict` answers "can earn" (trains + a station), which is
  the right question at *Routes* and the wrong one at *Dividends*: once the Routes step is behind it with
  nothing run, there is nothing to declare. **Rule:** at Dividends with `routes_run_this_turn === 0`, force
  the withhold — a derived action, so the server generates it.

### 18a/b/c — the mini-chart, third report

#1268 replaced the CSS transition with explicit keyframes and the symptom is unchanged: *teleport ~3 cells
right, slide back ~1.5, stop on a divider*. The "1.5 cell" and "on a divider" are the tell: **the offsets are
in a different pixel space from the grid.** The dialog is inside the 0.63 chrome zoom; a `translate(px)` on
a transform inside `zoom` is *not* scaled the way layout lengths are in current Chrome — 3 cells ≈ 47px ×
(1/0.63 − 1) × 5 ≈ the reported jump. The Stock Market tab is unaffected because its tokens are laid out
with `top`/`left`, not transforms. Not B&O's cell (18c) — it lands on a divider, which no cell arithmetic
produces. **Fix:** position the moving token with `left`/`top` (animate those; 120 cells is cheap) and stop
using `transform` anywhere under the zoom, or counter-zoom the preview like `boardPane`. The pay panel
"beginning on the destination cell" (18b) is the `restingNode` fallback drawing the answer when `animate()`
is unavailable — check what the panel passes.

### 16 / 14 — the merge was never a merge

The dividend machine's "fall" (#1082) is a 26px drop-and-fade of the payer row **while its grid track
collapses to zero**; the total flips at the merge mark. Built to spec, but what the eye sees is the row
vanishing and the panel getting shorter — exactly the report. The treasury machine copies it, so #14 is the
same fault plus (probably) a second dispatch in the same burst replacing the event (`token` remount) before
the 3.5 s ran out. **One animation, both machines:** the figure itself travels to the total's baseline (FLIP
on the amount span), the total counts to the new value on impact, the row height does not change until the
leave. Queue events rather than replace them. #16a inverts the direction for the treasury.

### 24 — the digest disagreed on `public_companies`

The client's values are printed; the server's are not. Replay the export through
`node dist/server/src/replayCli.js` (it prints `public_companies` at 150) and diff against the client dump in
the report. Candidates from the dump: `last_completed_run_revenue` present on B&O/C&O but absent on PRR after
a run; `station_tokens` vs `station_token_hexes` for PRR (a herald-home again). Until diffed, this is not a
rule bug — it is the alarm doing its job after a refresh at 150 ("not agreed at any point" is literal: the
first comparison was the reload).

### 2 — the picker should not reload

Ruled against. That means the runtime refactor #1273 avoided: `UI_SCALE` as a CSS custom property
(`--ui-scale`) with `zoom: var(--ui-scale)` and `calc(1 / var(--ui-scale))` for the counter-zooms, the JS
readers (`statusDockHeight`, the action bar clearance, the treasury panel's `top`) reading a live getter.
`uiScale.test.ts` pins the constant and will be rewritten with it. Doing this first also fixes 18a, if the
preview is counter-zoomed rather than patched.
